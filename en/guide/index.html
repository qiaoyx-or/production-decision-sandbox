What do you provide, and what do you get?

Your organization supplies business facts and management judgment. DecisioWorks organizes those inputs into a computational process and returns results for planners or business applications. Commercial deployments can use this process in ongoing operations; a focused first scenario establishes shared definitions.

Inputs and outputs of a production decision

Business dataOrders, products, routes, resources, calendars, and the materials or inventory needed by the scenarioShared objects, mappings and relationship checks
