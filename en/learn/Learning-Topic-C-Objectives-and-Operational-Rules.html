Topic

C Objectives, Experience and Operational Rules

On this page
Preparation and OutcomesFurther Reading

Separate facts, constraints, preferences and measures requiring approval, then express them through data, objectives and rule interfaces. Explain the trade-offs and their practical effects.

Preparation and Outcomes

Read B1: Business Objects and Fields and A3: A Common Comparison Basis before distinguishing facts, hard conditions, soft conditions and preferences.

The reading exercises produce a requirements table, an objective definition and an operational-rule record. Testing whether a rule affects solving additionally requires a configuration and runtime that support it.

  1. Distinguish Facts, Constraints and Preferences
  2. Explain the Trade-Offs between Objectives
  3. Turn Shop-Floor Experience into Checkable Rules

Read the three lessons in order. Attempt each exercise before checking the suggested answer, and use the worksheets to record conditions, results and open questions.
