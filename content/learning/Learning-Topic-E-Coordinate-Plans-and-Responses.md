# E Coordinate Planning Levels and Responses

[中文](Learning-Topic-E-Coordinate-Plans-and-Responses-zh-CN.md) · [Learning Center](Learning-Center.md)

Connect planning levels through release conditions, deviations and signals. Clarify what each level owns, what it passes on and how the effects of a change are reviewed.

## Preparation and Outcomes
Review [B3](Learning-B3-Align-Time-Capacity-and-Materials.md) and [C1](Learning-C1-Facts-Constraints-and-Preferences.md) for time, materials and constraints. Release quantities and outage responses can first be studied through classroom calculations and discussion.

Produce a release checklist, an explanation of a change's planning impact and a task-response record. Running a linkage recipe additionally requires checking its inputs, receiving actions and result states against the current interface.

1. [From the Master Plan to Work Release and Scheduling](Learning-E1-Master-Plan-to-Work-Release.md)
2. [Express Changes as Plan Deviations and Signals](Learning-E2-Deviations-to-Planning-Signals.md)
3. [Define the Scope of a Disruption Response](Learning-E3-Scope-a-Disruption-Response.md)

Read the three lessons in order. Attempt each exercise before checking the suggested answer, and use the worksheets to record conditions, results and open questions.
