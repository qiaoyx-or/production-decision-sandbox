# H Build Everyday Decision Capability

[中文](Learning-Topic-H-Build-Everyday-Decision-Capability-zh-CN.md) · [Learning Center](Learning-Center.md)

Keep reviewable conclusions from each run and build the next adjustment on previous experience. Cover result review, ongoing operation and cross-functional learning.

## Preparation and Outcomes
Read [A3](Learning-A3-Compare-Plans-on-the-Same-Basis.md), then choose [D1's stamping case](Learning-D1-Read-a-Stamping-Plan.md) or [D2's injection case](Learning-D2-Read-Injection-Molding-Changeovers.md) as shared evidence. Their tables support factual review and discussion even without local run records.

Finish with a result-review record, an operating checklist and follow-up actions with owners. Identify the records supporting each decision and any facts still needed.

1. [Review a Plan from Run Status to Business Conclusion](Learning-H1-Review-a-Plan.md)
2. [Move from a Small Trial to Continued Operation](Learning-H2-Move-to-Continued-Operation.md)
3. [Run a Cross-Functional Plan Review](Learning-H3-Run-a-Cross-Functional-Review.md)

Read the three lessons in order. Attempt each exercise before checking the suggested answer, and use the worksheets to record conditions, results and open questions.
