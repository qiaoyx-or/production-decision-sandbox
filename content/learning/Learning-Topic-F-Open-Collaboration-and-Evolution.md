# F Open Collaboration and Continued Evolution

[中文](Learning-Topic-F-Open-Collaboration-and-Evolution-zh-CN.md) · [Learning Center](Learning-Center.md)

Place changes in the appropriate layer and preserve data, rules and workflows as reusable assets. Manufacturers, consultants and software partners collaborate through shared interfaces.

## Preparation and Outcomes
Bring the decision task from [A1](Learning-A1-Define-a-Decision-Task.md), or use this topic's color-grouping and workshop-transfer examples. [B1's data mapping](Learning-B1-Business-Tables-to-Computable-Objects.md) helps with the discussion of template reuse.

This topic focuses on responsibilities and design choices, without requiring execution. Produce a change-impact table, template reuse notes and a handoff table that another participant can act on.

1. [Locate the Layer That Should Handle a Change](Learning-F1-Place-Changes-in-the-Right-Layer.md)
2. [Preserve Experience in Data Assets and Industry Templates](Learning-F2-Preserve-Data-Assets-and-Templates.md)
3. [Define Roles for Manufacturers, Consultants and Software Partners](Learning-F3-Define-Ecosystem-Responsibilities.md)

Read the three lessons in order. Attempt each exercise before checking the suggested answer, and use the worksheets to record conditions, results and open questions.
