# B Represent Manufacturing in Data

[中文](Learning-Topic-B-Manufacturing-Data-zh-CN.md) · [Learning Center](Learning-Center.md)

Trace orders through products, operations, resources and materials, then align them on a common time axis. Learn to inspect business meaning as well as column names.

## Preparation and Outcomes
Identify the objects and planning period first; [A1: Define a Decision Task](Learning-A1-Define-a-Decision-Task.md) can help. The unit, material and time calculations in the text can be completed without a dataset.

Produce a field map, operation-material relationships and time-alignment checks. An integration exercise additionally needs a data copy and its field definitions. Checks should identify specific objects and issues.

1. [From Business Tables to Computable Objects](Learning-B1-Business-Tables-to-Computable-Objects.md)
2. [Process Routes and Operation-Level BOMs](Learning-B2-Routes-and-Operation-Level-BOM.md)
3. [Align Time, Capacity and Material Availability](Learning-B3-Align-Time-Capacity-and-Materials.md)

Read the three lessons in order. Attempt each exercise before checking the suggested answer, and use the worksheets to record conditions, results and open questions.
