# D Read Plans through Recorded Cases

[中文](Learning-Topic-D-Read-Real-Planning-Cases-zh-CN.md) · [Learning Center](Learning-Center.md)

Read the published v1.4.0 stamping and injection-molding records through inputs, configuration, execution and results. Then assess other cases by their data characteristics.

## Preparation and Outcomes
Use [A3](Learning-A3-Compare-Plans-on-the-Same-Basis.md) to distinguish scores from business measures. See [C1](Learning-C1-Facts-Constraints-and-Preferences.md) for soft capacity and [C2](Learning-C2-Explain-Objective-Tradeoffs.md) for the color and capacity objectives.

D1 and D2 include reading exercises answerable from their tables. Object-level tracing requires detailed output, and a single-variable comparison requires a new run. D3 supplies scenarios X and Y for practice before you profile your own datasets.

1. [Stamping Planning: Read Demand, Capacity and Materials Together](Learning-D1-Read-a-Stamping-Plan.md)
2. [Injection Molding: Explain Attributes and Changeovers](Learning-D2-Read-Injection-Molding-Changeovers.md)
3. [Choose Cases by Their Data Characteristics](Learning-D3-Select-Cases-by-Data-Characteristics.md)

Read the three lessons in order. Attempt each exercise before checking the suggested answer, and use the worksheets to record conditions, results and open questions.
